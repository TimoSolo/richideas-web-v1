About us

Rich Ideas is a family-focused, fee-based financial planning practice in Jukskei Park, Johannesburg. We work with parents and families across South Africa and abroad, mostly online, and always on one principle: financial planning first, not policy sales.

We’re proudly independent. We don’t push products to earn commission. Instead, we charge a clear fee to build your personal financial plan, then walk the journey with you through our retainer plans so the plan keeps changing as your life does. You’ll always know what you’re paying for, and why it matters.

Managing money can feel overwhelming, confusing and even stressful. That’s where we come in. We listen, we simplify, and we guide. Whether you’re starting out, growing your family, changing careers, building a business or planning for retirement, we help you make money choices that give you more confidence and peace of mind.

How we work with you

Financial planning doesn’t have to be complicated. Here’s how we walk the journey with you, step by step.

  1. Let’s connect: Do We Fit? A short virtual chat, at our expense, to see if we’re the right fit. We get to know each other, hear your concerns and share how we work.
  2. Foundation session/s. We listen carefully to understand your goals, values and financial picture. You share documents, complete our client questionnaire and tell us what matters most.
  3. Making sense of it all. Behind the scenes we do the number crunching and research: analysis, projections, comparisons and recommendations tailored to you.
  4. Your financial roadmap. We meet again to walk through your personalised plan. Clear, actionable steps you can take right away, and support to help you do it.
  5. Implementation. We help put the plan into action: settling debt, tax strategies, setting up investments, sorting out insurance, drafting a Will, cashflow planning.
  6. Ongoing guidance. As life changes, so should your plan. We meet regularly, depending on your plan, and stay in touch to keep it on track.

Our plans and fees are published on the Fee Structure page and in our Services Guide.
